A quick Mediterranean cruise

Five 7-day options and two longer alternatives, built only from our November 3 crawl of princess.com. Prices are Princess's "from" fares per person, as shown on the destination pages that day.

Short answer: for the best value on a week away, take Mediterranean & Adriatic (N720) from $1,094. For Greek islands, take Italy & Turkey (U740) from $1,254. If you can stretch to 11 days, Greek Isles & Adriatic (N726) costs about the same as a 7-day cruise ($1,499) and is the cheapest per day.
